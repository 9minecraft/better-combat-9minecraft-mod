# Better Combat — Hit Multiple Enemies at Once

Better Combat 9minecraft Mod overhauls how you fight in the game. Instead of clicking one target at a time, this mod gives every weapon a unique swing animation. You can hit multiple enemies with a single strike, just like in action games. It turns basic battles into fun, fast-paced fights where your movement and timing matter.

Hit Multiple Targets: A single swing damages every enemy caught inside your weapon's reach.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Molnár Zsolt**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
